🌱 Beginner guide — from scratch to pro in 15 minutes

0. The one idea: capital (how much to start) × growth (how fast) × break-even (how many sales). All else is commentary.
1. Start cost: 2,000 ÷ 5,750 = 0.348. Test with 2,000 (friends try it, 3 videos, 200–300 ads). Launch with 8–40k, compliant.
2. Growth: (30,000,000 ÷ 2,000)^(1/8) − 1 = 232.67%. Market ~19%. 12.25× faster. 55.3 years at market. Extraordinary.
3. Quit rule: net = 4 × salary for 12 worst-months in a row. 20k salary → 80k net = 20 you + 20 growth + 20 run + 20 buffer.
4. Profit: not in 7 days. Runway 18–24 months. 80–90% fail year one. Cover costs → break-even → profit.
5. Product + channel: time-saver + 10-second demo + margin ~60 after fees. Instagram checkout (UAE), Snapchat (Saudi). Phones ~78.67%.
